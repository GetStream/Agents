An agent config can keep its tools and instructions cached for an hour: `prompt_cache_ttl`, the `PromptCacheTtl` enum (`5m`, `1h`). `AgentConfig`, `AgentConfigRequest`, `AgentConfigPatch` and `SyncAgentRequest` gained it; left out on a sync or patch, the stored one stays, and a read leaves it out when the agent keeps the provider's default. agent.yaml's top-level `prompt_cache_ttl: 1h` is sent as the sync's `prompt_cache_ttl`. Go has it (`acceleration.PromptCacheTtl1h`, `agents.Settings.PromptCacheTTL`). Other SDKs need to update: regenerate the models, and read `prompt_cache_ttl` from agent.yaml into the sync body.
