`createGuestUser` (`POST /v1/agents/guests`) now answers two more refusals (AI-3734). A 429 `rate_limited` is one address (an IPv6 address counted by its /64) minting more than 60 new guests of an app in a clock hour; its `Retry-After` says how many seconds to wait. A guest coming back with its `refresh_secret` or its own token is not counted, and neither is the app's backend. A 403 `account_suspended` is an organization that is suspended. Go, JavaScript and Python (`plugins/stream`) have the regenerated clients. Dart reads `Retry-After` into `RouterException.retryAfterSeconds` on every refusal, as Kotlin's `AgentsException.Http.retryAfterSeconds` does, and its `guestUser` names both refusals. Other SDKs need to update: a client-side guest helper (Swift, Kotlin, Dart) should keep and reuse the guest it was minted rather than mint one per launch, and surface the 429 as a wait rather than a failure to retry at once.
