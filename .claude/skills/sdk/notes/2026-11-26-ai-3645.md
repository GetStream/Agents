The statistics endpoints refuse a window wider than their limit (AI-3645): `getTurnStats`, `getStats`, `getTagStats` and `getSpend` now answer 400 `invalid_request` for a window wider than 31 days (granularity hourly, the default) or 400 days (daily), counted up to now. The limit is in the description of `to`. Go, JavaScript, Rust and Python (`plugins/stream`) have the regenerated clients. The generated files of the other SDKs do not carry the description, so they need no change.
