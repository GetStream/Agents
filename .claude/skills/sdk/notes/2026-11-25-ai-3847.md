A dispatch worker's frame over 5 MiB is dropped rather than acted on (no spec change): the router answers it on the dispatch socket with `{"type": "error", "error", "code": "payload_too_large", "error_type": "payload_too_large"}`, fails the hosted call when it was a `tool_result`, gives the work its room back when it was a `done`, and keeps the worker. Go's `Dispatch` logs the `error` frame as a warning. Other SDKs need to update: JavaScript, Python (`plugins/stream`), .NET, Ruby, Rust and PHP ignore the frame, and log it as Go does. Swift, Kotlin and Dart have no dispatch worker.
