Since AI-3990 the agent's scheduling tools wait on their step for the user to allow them (`schedule_task`, and a reschedule, snooze, resume, or new title or instructions through `update_scheduled_task`), as a connector call under `require_approval` already did. Such a step is server-run: it comes as an `ai_tool_call` step with status `awaiting_approval` in the conversation, never as a `tool_call` frame, and is answered with `{"type": "tool_approval", "tool_call_id": <the step's id>, "request_id": <the reply's request_id>, "allowed": true|false}` on the session's events socket. Unanswered for 10 minutes, it is declined.

Go answers it with `Pipeline.AnswerApproval`, JS with `AgentChat.decide` (`decideShown`) and Kotlin with `AgentSession.decide(ShownToolCall)`. Swift (`decide(_: ShownToolCall, ...)`, #2538) and Dart (`decideShown`, #2542) do it in their own PRs. The TUI (`tui/`) shows no approval at all, connector approvals included.

The `ToolApprovalCommand` descriptions in the spec now name the scheduling tools beside `require_approval`. Go, JS and Python (`plugins/stream`) are regenerated. .NET, Ruby, Rust and PHP need their generated models regenerated; no code change.
