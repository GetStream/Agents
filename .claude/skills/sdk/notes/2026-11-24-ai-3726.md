An end user's device may no longer choose a new session's phone line, models, limits, greeting or cost labels (AI-3726, AI-3728): `createSession` and the session socket's `start` frame answer 403 (`permission`) to a device sending `phone`, `llm`, `stt`, `tts`, `sts`, `search`, `voice`, `max_tokens`, `max_tool_rounds`, `model_overwrites`, `greeting` or `tags`, as they already did for `history` and `instructions`. A backend sends them as before. JavaScript, Swift and Python (`plugins/stream`) have the regenerated clients, and the example apps no longer name a chat model. Other SDKs need to update: a client-side SDK (Swift, Kotlin, Dart, the browser side of JavaScript) should drop those fields from the session options a device opens a session with (`llm`, `stt`, `tts`, `voice`, `greeting`, `tags`, `modelOverwrites` in Swift's and Kotlin's `SessionOptions`, `llm` in Dart's), or document them as server-side only. Server-side SDKs are unaffected.
