Updating, pausing or resuming a scheduled task (`updateScheduledTask`, `pauseScheduledTask`, `resumeScheduledTask`) can now answer 409 `conflict` when the task changed between the router's read and its save: another edit or pause, or a worker claiming, holding or finishing an occurrence (AI-3496). The request changed nothing, and reading the task again and retrying is the answer. Go's generated client has the new `JSON409` fields, and the Python plugin and JavaScript types are regenerated. Other SDKs need to update: .NET, Ruby, Rust, PHP, Swift, Kotlin and Dart surface the 409 as their conflict error and document the retry on those three methods.
