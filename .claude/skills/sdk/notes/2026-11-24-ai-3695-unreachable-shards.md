A router of several shards answers `listCalls` (`GET /v1/agents/calls`) and `getCall` (`GET /v1/agents/calls/{id}`) with a 200 while a shard is down, naming the shards that did not answer in the `X-Unreachable-Shards` response header, comma-separated (AI-3695). On the list, the calls of those shards are missing; on a call, the models it used and its usage are left out when they could not all be read. The header is absent when every shard answered, and a browser may read it (it is in `Access-Control-Expose-Headers`). Bodies are unchanged. Go reads it from the generated client's `ListCallsResponse200Headers.XUnreachableShards` and `GetCallResponse200Headers.XUnreachableShards`; JavaScript has it typed in `sdks/js/src/generated/api.ts`. Other SDKs need to update: a call list or call read that hands back the calls should also hand back the unreachable shards (an empty list when the header is absent), so an app can say the list is incomplete instead of showing a short one as whole.
