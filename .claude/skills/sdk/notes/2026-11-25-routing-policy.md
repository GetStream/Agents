An app and an agent config choose where their work may be placed: `routing_policy`, the `RoutingPolicy` enum (`closest`, `us_east`, `us_only`, `eu_only`, `canada_only`, or empty). `AppSettings` (`getAppSettings`) gained `routing_policy`, `closest` when the app never set one, and the new `updateAppSettings` (`PATCH /v1/settings/app`, body `UpdateAppSettings`) writes it, empty meaning `closest`. `AgentConfig`, `AgentConfigRequest` and `AgentConfigPatch` gained an optional `routing_policy` that wins over the app's: empty follows the app's, left out on an update keeps the stored one, and it is absent from a read when the agent follows the app. Go has them generated as `acceleration.RoutingPolicy` (constants `RoutingPolicyClosest` and so on, `RoutingPolicyUnset` for empty) and `UpdateAppSettingsWithResponse`. Other SDKs need to update: regenerate the models and the settings operation.
