`ConnectionInvocation.error_message` (`GET /v1/agents/connections/{id}/invocations`) no longer carries the provider's error body or the call's error (AI-3764). A call the router held itself keeps the router's words, such as `declined by the person` or `rate limited by the router: retry after 30s`; any other failure is `the call failed: <error_type>`, and what the provider answered is only in the router's log under the call's id. Only the field's description changed in the spec. Go has it through the regenerated `acceleration.ConnectionInvocation` (`sdks/go/acceleration/generated.go`), Python through the regenerated `ConnectionInvocation` and JavaScript through the regenerated `Schemas["ConnectionInvocation"]`. Other SDKs need to update: .NET, Ruby, Rust and PHP take the new description when they regenerate. Swift, Kotlin and Dart have no invocations operation, which is server-side only.
